Read the measures

Statutory OTP
Terminal arrival from −59 seconds through +359 seconds against schedule, inclusive. −60 is Early; +360 is Late.
Freeway runtime
An IVOMS travel-time measure or proxy between verified boundaries. Runtime difference is observed minus scheduled runtime; it is not an independent traffic-congestion measurement.
Physical phases and direction
P&R connection, freeway and destination connection retain their physical meaning on reverse trips. Boundary evidence must be verified for each route and direction.
Empty service and deadhead
Any positive load, boarding or alighting establishes passenger presence. Empty requires all these fields to be explicitly zero; missing values remain unknown. Deadhead requires a source movement classification.
Time slices
Scheduled stop-pair departure in Central Time: Early AM 04–05, AM Peak 06–08, Morning 09–11, Mid-Day 12–13, Afternoon 14–15, PM Peak 16–17, After PM 18–19, Evening 20–21, Late 22–03. Overnight remains visible. This resolves the documented pilot fallback discrepancy provisionally, pending pilot review.
Cutover and coverage
Each pair is classified by actual arrival; a pair crossing 9 PM is impact service and its full runtime is flagged. Trips crossing the cutoff retain both per-pair periods and a trip flag. No interpolation of miles or time across the cutoff is implied. Baseline starts provisionally August 1, 2026.
